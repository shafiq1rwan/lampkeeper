# Lampkeeper visual reference

`lampkeeper-mockup.png` is the concept image discussed with the owner. It was generated with the built-in image generation tool, not captured from a running game. Use it for composition and art direction; `../../prompt.md` defines the actual mechanics and intentionally clarifies the inspection lantern's role.

The owner expects to revise the visuals later. Keep this image out of the runtime bundle.

## Original image generation prompt

```text
Use case: ui-mockup.
Create one polished 16:9 landscape in-game screenshot concept for LAMPKEEPER, an original small browser suspense game playable on phones and desktop. This is a believable achievable indie game visual target, rendered entirely in a cohesive procedural voxel style: visibly cubic block-built characters, modular block walls, chunky furniture, low geometry, flat color materials, crisp edges. No photorealism, no illustrated concept art brushwork.

Camera: readable orthographic three-quarter overhead view of a cutaway apartment floor, fairly close so the player and threat are readable on a phone. The playable floor fills most of the image; crop the building into the edges rather than showing an isolated floating miniature. Front-facing obstructing walls removed. Three small furnished apartment rooms connect to an L-shaped hallway with a breaker closet and an exit door. Sparse environmental details: doormats, numbered doors, houseplants, a sofa, a wall clock, breaker wiring.

Central scene: a small voxel caretaker wearing a mustard yellow work jacket and carrying a glowing portable lamp stands at a corridor junction. Warm amber pools of light form a clear safe route from the character toward the left-hand rooms. The corridor leading to the right is cool desaturated navy and teal, dim but spatially readable. At the edge of the darkness is one lanky original blocky shadow creature, visible silhouette, small pale eyes, halted immediately outside a warm light pool. No gore, no attack, playful eerie suspense. One unlit ceiling fixture ahead has a subtle small repair icon. A visible wall-mounted breaker panel has two lit switches and one unlit switch. A gentle dashed amber path indicates the next repair objective without overwhelming the environment.

Lighting: strong visual distinction between cozy amber safe light and cool blue darkness, soft ambient visibility in all rooms, modest bloom only around bulbs. Attractive restricted palette, clean contact shadows, feasible game-engine shading. Avoid excessive fog, cinematic depth of field, tiny decorative clutter, or complex realistic reflections.

Overlay restrained high-quality flat game UI with clear sans-serif lettering: small upper-left label "LAMPKEEPER", beneath it "Repair lights  1 / 3". Top-center compact power indicator "POWER  2 / 3" with three simple segmented bars, two amber and one empty. Top-right small pause icon. Bottom-left translucent virtual thumbstick; bottom-right one large round amber interaction button with a white lightbulb icon and small label "REPAIR". Leave the central action unobscured. No health bar, no currency, no additional action buttons. This is the mobile landscape gameplay view, not a phone device mockup, not a poster, not a title screen, not a collage. Make the image feel inviting to play and clearly communicate: light is safety; darkness contains danger.
```
